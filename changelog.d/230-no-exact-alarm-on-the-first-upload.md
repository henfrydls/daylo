**On Android, the daily reminder arrives around the time you chose, and Daylo no longer
asks for permission to set exact alarms.** Since 1.5.1 the settings screen could take you
to the phone's "Alarms & reminders" screen so the reminder arrived on the minute. That
permission is not part of this version, so the screen no longer offers the way there. The
reminder still comes every evening; Android picks the exact moment, usually close to the
time you set, and later if the phone has been asleep.
