**Moving around with the keyboard now stays inside whatever is open.**
With a day, Settings or another panel open, pressing Tab or Shift+Tab used to wander off
into the calendar behind it. Now the first press goes into the panel and every press after
that moves to the next button or field inside it, until you close it. This holds on a Mac
too, where Tab used to skip buttons and slip out, and on Linux, where Shift+Tab slipped out
of panels and left the menu open. Closing a panel with a tap outside it puts you back on the
button that opened it, without leaving an outline around it.
