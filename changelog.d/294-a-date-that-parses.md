**Ticking a day no longer leaves a blank screen for people whose saved data came from an
older backup.**
A backup could carry dates written in a form Daylo itself never uses, such as "2026/11/01".
Daylo accepted them when the backup was brought in, and later the whole window went white the
moment you ticked a day. Those records now open as usual and keep everything they held, with
their dates saved the way Daylo writes them. The same happens when you bring in a backup that
has them, so a backup made before this fix still comes back in whole.
