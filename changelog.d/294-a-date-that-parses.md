**Ticking a day no longer leaves a blank window when your saved data holds certain old
dates.**
Records brought in from some older backups could carry dates written in a way Daylo itself
never uses, such as "2026/11/01". The app opened as usual, but the moment you ticked a day
the whole window went white. Now ticking works as it should, and those records, and the
backups that hold them, are kept exactly as they are.
