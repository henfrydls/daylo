**On Android, Daylo offers the daily reminder on your second day, for nine in the evening.**
It used to ask the moment you made your first habit, over the day you were logging, and
suggest the next hour. Now it waits until you come back on another day and tick something,
and asks once nothing else is open. Closing it without answering no longer counts as a no:
it asks again on a later day, and after the third time it stops. "Not now" still means no.
