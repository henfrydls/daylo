**On Android, Daylo offers the daily reminder on your second day, for nine in the evening.**
It used to ask the moment you made your first habit, over the day you were logging, and
suggest the next hour. Now it waits until you come back and log a second day, asks once you
close that day, and closing it without answering no longer counts as a no: it asks again
another time. "Not now" still means no.
