**Everything there is to decide is in one place, called Settings.** Export and import, the
daily reminder on Android, the anonymous check-in with the whole message it sends written
out, whether Daylo looks for new versions, and who made it. On a screen with room it opens
as a panel beside the calendar; on a phone it takes the screen.

The menu of six entries is gone with it. Where there is nothing to choose between, the
corner of the header is the gear itself and one press opens Settings; on Android, where the
daily reminder is a thing you can be taken straight to, it is still a menu, of two.

The version lives in Settings now, under About, and pressing the row asks whether there is a
newer one. Installing is the small word beside the number and nothing else: a row is easy to
press by accident, and installing closes Daylo and opens it again.
