**On Android, Daylo offers the daily reminder for nine in the evening, once your first day is in.**
It still asks as soon as you have your first activity, but now it waits until you close
that day, instead of asking over the day you were logging, and it suggests nine in the
evening instead of the next hour. Closing it without answering no longer counts as a no:
it asks again on a later day, after you log something, and after the third time it stops.
"Not now" still means no.
