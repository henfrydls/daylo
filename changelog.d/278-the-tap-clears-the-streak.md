**On Android, tapping the daily reminder now shows today on top, even with your streak open.**
If your streak was open when you tapped the reminder, today's list opened underneath it, out
of sight, and the back button closed the list before you had seen it. Now the streak closes
and today's list is what you see.

**Daylo no longer offers the daily reminder to someone who has already turned it on.**
If you turned the reminder on in Settings, the offer could still ask you about it, and it
could ask again after you turned the reminder off. Now turning it on or off in Settings
counts as your answer, and you are not asked.
