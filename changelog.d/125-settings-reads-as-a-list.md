**Settings reads as a list rather than a ledger.** The grey rules between every two rows are
gone; what tells one row from the next is the space around it. The anonymous check-in is a
single line with its switch, and everything it is for, everything it sends and what turning
it off costs are together behind What gets sent, in one place instead of three.

**Send feedback is a row you can press.** It was a word at the foot of the screen, three
sections below the sentence describing it, and on daylo.henfrydls.com pressing it did
nothing at all and said nothing either: the app was waiting for the browser to follow a link
that was not a link. Inside Daylo it still asks you there rather than handing you a letter.

The privacy policy went the other way, down to the links at the foot beside the website, the
source and the license.
