**The month view says Today once, and the Today bar now brings you back to this month.**
The month used to show a Today button next to the arrows as well as the Today bar, and in
this month the button did nothing. The button is gone, and the arrows now sit beside the
month's name. When you look at another month, the Today bar stays in its place, in white
with today's date; tap it to come back to this month. On your first day, before you add an
activity, the year view no longer shows a second Today next to "Add your first activity".
