**Daylo now notices that a new day has started, even if you never closed it.** Before, if you ticked your habit in the evening and left Daylo open overnight, the next morning it still showed yesterday as today, with your habit already marked as done. Now it moves to the new day as soon as you come back to it, or at midnight if you are looking at it. If you are in the middle of writing something at midnight, it waits until you have finished.
