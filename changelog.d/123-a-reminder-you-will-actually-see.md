**The daily reminder is offered for later today, not for a time somebody chose years ago.**
When Daylo asks whether to remind you, it now proposes the next hour on the hour, so a
reminder agreed to at ten to nine is one that arrives at nine. From ten at night until six
in the morning it offers eight in the evening instead, because the next hour there is a
notification nobody wants. If you had already picked a time yourself, that is still the one
it offers.
