**Opening Daylo twice no longer makes one window erase what you ticked in the other.**
Before, if you opened Daylo a second time, ticked today there and closed it, the first
window still remembered the day as it was. The next thing you did in that first window
saved its old copy over yours, and today was unticked again without a word. Now a window
picks up what the other one saved before it saves anything of its own.
