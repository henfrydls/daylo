**When Daylo cannot save, it now tells you in English and the warning stays until you
close it.** Before, if your device refused to save, for example because it was full, a
red note appeared in Spanish, vanished after three seconds and never came back that
session, even if saving kept failing later. Now it says what to do, open Settings and
export a backup, and waits for you. It goes away by itself once saving works again, and
if you close it while saving keeps failing, it tells you again five minutes later.
