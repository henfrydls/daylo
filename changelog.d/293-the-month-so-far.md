**The month view now says how the month is going, right by its name.**
Under the month's name on a phone, and next to it on a computer, a short line reads "17 of
25 days so far": the days you did something, out of the days the month has had. A month
that is over reads "20 of 31 days", and it follows you as you move between months. When a
month has no days done yet, the line stays out of the way. The month you started using
Daylo now counts from your first day, so on day one with everything done it says "1 of 1"
instead of "1 of 25", and the year you started counts from that day too. "This Month" in
the statistics, that month's card in the year view and the year's progress bar count the
same way, so they all agree. Deleting an activity no longer changes how your earlier months
are counted.
