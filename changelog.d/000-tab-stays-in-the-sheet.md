**Moving around with the keyboard now stays inside whatever is open.**
With a day, Settings or another panel open, pressing Tab or Shift+Tab used to wander off
into the calendar behind it. Now the first press goes into the panel and every press after
that stays there until you close it. On Linux, Shift+Tab also stops at the edge of the panel
instead of slipping out. Closing a panel with a tap outside it puts you back on the button
that opened it, without leaving an outline around it.
